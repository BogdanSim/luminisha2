## Users
Romanian pet owners (cats and dogs) shopping online for a feeding stand that fits a modern, tidy home. Some have older pets that struggle to bend down.

## Product Purpose
LUMINISHA sells laser-cut steel objects for pets. This surface is the product page for Mowgli, an ergonomic raised feeder stand with two stainless bowls, sold in three sizes (S, M, L).

## Positioning
Objects made by CNC laser cutting: controlled light shapes the metal. Function first, form follows. Pet products that belong in the home instead of looking like generic pet gear.

## Capabilities and Constraints
- Variable product: S, M, L. Each size changes dimensions, gallery and price.
- S: 33 x 18,5 cm, height 6 cm front / 9 cm back, cut-out 12 cm, bowls 14 cm.
- M: 37,5 x 20,5 cm, height 10 / 13 cm, cut-out 14 cm, bowls 16 cm, 1,7 kg with bowls.
- L: 46,5 x 25 cm, height 15 / 18 cm, cut-out 19 cm, bowls 21 cm, 2,7 kg with bowls.
- Carbon steel ~2 mm, electrostatic paint, matte black, laser-cut LM logo. Two stainless bowls included in every size (user confirmed).
- Open: prices (placeholders used), stock, delivery/production times, S weight. The spec sheets' "distance between centres" values are geometrically inconsistent and are not shown.
